INSTAGRAM / ZERNIO

Posting & auto-DMs

Real integrations via Zernio's API, requiring your authorized Instagram professional account.

Not connected. No Instagram messages or posts will be sent until your credentials and account permissions are configured.

1. Connect accounts

Create your Zernio account, connect Instagram via official OAuth, then configure ZERNIO_API_KEY and OWNER_ACCESS_TOKEN in this Vercel project's environment variables. Never paste your API key into a public webpage or chat.

Open Zernio ↗Vercel settings ↗

2. Your Instagram account

Only accounts legitimately connected in Zernio can post or message. Creator or Business account features/permissions vary.

3. Publish / schedule a post

Instagram posts generally require publicly accessible media. A scheduled post is sent to Zernio's scheduler, not this browser's calendar.

4. Comment → DM automation

Created paused for safety; enable only after checking that the DM is accurate and appropriate.

5. Inbox / replies

Load conversations from the connected Instagram account and send replies to an existing conversation only. No unsolicited DM blasting.

Limits & security

All network writes require an explicit confirmation. Real-time automation happens through Zernio after you activate a rule; this dashboard does not poll Instagram itself. Posting or DMs are impossible until your Instagram OAuth and private server keys are configured. ChatGPT Plus does not provide a backend API key.
